Clinical Synthesis

Leo's profile presents a classic **high-variance "spiky" profile**. The 50 point variance between Sensory (90) and Executive (40) domains indicates that environmental factors (noise, lighting) have a disproportionately high impact on his ability to deploy his cognitive strengths.

Domain Evaluation & Analysis

Cognitive Strength Analysis

Detailed testing shows that while **Sequential Logic** (step-by-step thinking) is a struggle, **Global Synthesis** (big picture thinking) is in the top 5th percentile.

Implication: Leo solves problems by seeing the final outcome intuitively rather than following a prescribed path. Standard academic assessments that grade "show your work" may systematically undervalue his ability.

Executive Function Evaluation

Analysis of task initiation (Score: 35) suggests a **high-friction mental start-up**. Once engaged, sustained focus is variable but can be high (hyper-focus).

Intervention: Use "Low-Stakes Start" techniques (e.g., doing just the first 30 seconds of a task) to bypass the executive threshold barrier.

Sensory Integration Profile

The DNS Sensory score of 90 indicates **Hypersensitivity**. Leo experiences sensory input with 3-4x the intensity of neurotypical peers.

Classroom Adaptation: Noise-cancelling headphones and designated "sensory retreat" zones are non-negotiable for maintaining cognitive readiness.

Scale Methodology

The Deenz scale moves away from "deficits" and looks at **Processing Potentials**. A score of 100 doesn't mean "perfect," it means "highest intensity variation in this domain."
