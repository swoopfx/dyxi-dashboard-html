Model Overview

The Kirkpatrick Model helps us understand not just if Leo is "using" the app, but if it's actually changing his learning outcomes.

Level 1: Reaction

Leo's Engagement Analysis

Leo shows high initial enthusiasm (88%) when using the Visual Tracing Tool. However, his reaction to Phonetic Drills is more reserved, often requiring a 5-minute "warm-up" period. He responds best to the "Natural Tones" interface, which reduces his sensory overload.

Engagement and initial response to learning tools.

Level 2: Learning

Leo's Skill Acquisition

Over the last 30 days, Leo's Word Recognition has grown by 24%. He has successfully mastered 12 new "sight words." His Phonological Awareness is currently at 62%, showing steady improvement in vowel sound differentiation.

Increase in knowledge, skills, and confidence.

Level 3: Behavior

Application in Real-World

Leo has begun to independently reach for his "Reading Helper" overlay during homework. This behavior shift indicates he is internalizing the strategies learned in the dashboard sessions. His "Application Rate" of learned phonics in casual reading has reached 45%.

Application of learning in real-world scenarios.

Level 4: Results

Academic & Personal Impact

The primary result is a significant reduction in "Reading Frustration" during school hours. His teacher reports a more confident participation in group reading activities. Long-term goal: Grade-level reading proficiency by end of Year 4.

Final results and long-term impact of the intervention.
