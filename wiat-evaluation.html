What makes WIAT-4 Unique?

The **WIAT-4** is the gold standard for measuring academic achievement. Unlike other tests, it includes unique components like **Oral Expression** and **Sentence Composition**, providing a holistic view of how a student applies their knowledge in real-world academic tasks.

Problem Solving vs Calculation

Discrepancy Detected

Leo scored **86 in Mathematical Reasoning** but only **72 in Numerical Operations**. This suggests he understands math concepts well but struggles with the mechanical execution and math facts recall.

Sentence Composition

Significant Deficit

Score: **64**. Leo shows difficulty translating his advanced oral ideas into written sentences. His handwriting speed is slow, and punctuation/capitalization are inconsistent.

Oral Expression (Strength)

Score: **115**. This is Leo's highest achievement area. He can explain complex stories and vocabulary verbally far beyond his grade level. This is a crucial "compensatory skill" for him.

Clinician's Summary

Leo exhibits a classic **IQ-Achievement Discrepancy**. His high verbal intelligence (Oral Expression 115) is not translating into written output (Sentence Composition 64).

This gap is a primary indicator of a Specific Learning Disorder (SLD) in writing.

Interventions

Speech-to-Text software for writing tasks.

Explicit instruction in sentence structure.

Scaffolded math worksheets focusing on concepts over calculations.
